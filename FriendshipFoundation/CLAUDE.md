**Core mission statement:**

"Our mission is to help those who have lost their way find a new path to a new beginning."

**Full mission:**

At Friendship Foundation, we believe in the potential of every individual to contribute to a better world. We believe in empowering people to embrace their identity, define their future, and create positive change in their communities.

Everyone deserves the opportunity to rebuild their lives with dignity and purpose.

Our mission is to illuminate pathways toward opportunity, possibility, wellness, and recovery by supporting programs that empower individuals to overcome challenges and regain stability.

By fostering a sense of community and advocating for those in need, we strive to create pathways to stability, independence, and renewed purpose.

Individuals facing homelessness, addiction, mental illness, and related barriers to stability and recovery in Western New York.

## DONATION / FUNDRAISING — PRIORITY

**DONATION IS A PRIMARY PURPOSE OF THIS WEBSITE.**

Prominent DONATE opportunities are mandatory in ALL THREE design directions.

**Required donation UX elements in every mockup:**

- Prominent DONATE button in desktop navigation
- Prominent DONATE action on mobile
- DONATE CTA in or immediately adjacent to hero
- Contextual donation opportunity within homepage story
- Strong donation section toward bottom of homepage
- Final DONATE opportunity in footer

**Visitor flow:**

UNDERSTAND → CARE → SEE A PATH FORWARD → HELP → DONATE

**Supporting CTA language examples:**

- Donate
- Give Hope
- Help Create a New Beginning
- Help Someone Find a Path Forward
- Support the Mission

**Critical:** The word DONATE must remain clearly visible somewhere. Do not sacrifice conversion clarity for creative language.

**Donations should not be quarantined** to a /donate page. The case for giving should be woven naturally throughout the homepage. Visitors should understand WHY the work matters, WHO benefits, WHAT KIND OF CHANGE is possible, and HOW THEY CAN HELP before and while being invited to donate.

**Payment processing:** Do NOT create functioning financial transactions for these mockups. DONATE buttons may link to clearly identified placeholder destinations. Design the architecture so that Stripe, GoHighLevel, PayPal, or another solution can later be connected without redesigning the website.
